Privacy Policy

Idle Wizard Tower · Last updated: September 18, 2026

In short: this game does not require an account and does not collect your name, email, phone number, or location. Your game progress is saved only locally on your device. We only collect anonymous usage statistics (e.g. which features are used) to improve the game.

1. Who controls this data

Jakub Marczak · idle.wizard.support@gmail.com

2. What data we collect

Type of dataPurposeWhere it goes
Anonymous gameplay events (e.g. "session started", "chest opened", "achievement unlocked")App usage analysis, improving the gameGoogle Firebase Analytics
Basic device technical data (model, OS version, country) collected automatically by FirebaseAggregate statistics, diagnosticsGoogle Firebase Analytics

What we do NOT collect: your name, email address, phone number, GPS location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or any data that would allow us to directly identify you as a person.

3. Game progress storage

Your progress (mana, creature collection, achievements, etc.) is saved only locally on your device — we do not send it to any servers. If you uninstall the app, your progress will be lost unless your device has Google's automatic backup enabled.

4. Notifications

With your consent, the app sends local notifications (e.g. a reminder that your Treasure Chest is ready). These notifications are generated and handled entirely on your device — they do not require sending any data externally. You can withdraw this consent at any time in your phone's system settings.

7. Children

The app is not directed exclusively at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. 8. Your rights

Because the app does not collect data that directly identifies you, most classic requests (access, deletion, data portability) have no technical point of reference — we have no way to link the anonymous statistics we collect to a specific person. If you still have a privacy question, please contact: idle.wizard.support@gmail.com.

We may update this policy from time to time. The date of the last update is shown at the top of this page.
